    Privacy and data

    On this device

    Transcripts and timings are encrypted for your Windows account. Recordings are kept with your history so failed dictations can be retried. Delete any of them from History.

    Sent to cloud models

    Dictation audio and dictionary hints go to the model you choose (through OpenRouter, xAI or Vercel). Cleanup also sends the transcript and up to 500 nearby characters from the text field, never from password fields.

    How OpenRouter, GPT and MAI handle your data

    OpenRouter does not store inputs or outputs by default; logging is opt-in. OpenRouter policy.

    GPT-Transcribe. OpenAI lists no abuse-monitoring retention for audio transcription, and API content is not used for training. OpenAI API policy.

    MAI-Transcribe-2 runs on Azure fast transcription, which Microsoft says does not retain audio or transcripts. Microsoft Speech policy.

    Luna cleanup. OpenAI's text API may keep content up to 30 days for abuse monitoring. Review logging and zero data retention in OpenRouter privacy settings.

    Policies checked 3 October 2026.
