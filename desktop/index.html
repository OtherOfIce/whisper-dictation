Default to lock mode

When off, Ctrl + Win uses push-to-talk mode. Hold to speak and release to insert.

Voice model

Choose the model that turns recordings into text.

Grok sends audio continuously over a direct xAI WebSocket. Verbatim keeps fillers and false starts. Clean removes some spoken disfluencies. Luna cleanup, if enabled below, runs afterwards.

Text cleanup

Resolve spoken corrections and formatting instructions after transcription.

Adds a separate OpenRouter request before pasting. To fit the insertion, Luna also receives up to 500 nearby characters from the focused text field; password fields are excluded. Fast uses priority processing at twice the token price. If cleanup fails, the original transcript is used.

Dictionary

Paste your preferred names, spellings, and phrases, one per line. The full list is sent as recognition hints with each recording, even when cleanup is off.

Blank lines and duplicates are ignored. Grok accepts up to 100 terms of 50 characters each; other models accept the app limit of 1,000 terms and 12,000 characters. These are hints, not guaranteed replacements.

Saved on this device

Transcripts and timings are encrypted for your Windows account. Original WAV recordings are stored in the local history database. You can play, download, or delete them from History.
