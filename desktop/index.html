Text cleanup

Resolve spoken corrections and formatting instructions after transcription.

Adds a separate OpenRouter request before pasting. To fit the insertion, Luna also receives up to 500 nearby characters from the focused text field; password fields are excluded. Fast uses priority processing at twice the token price. If cleanup fails, the original transcript is used.

Dictionary

Paste your preferred names, spellings, and phrases, one per line. Models that support dictionary hints receive the list with each recording, even when cleanup is off.

Blank lines and duplicates are ignored. Grok accepts up to 100 terms of 50 characters each; other models accept the app limit of 1,000 terms and 12,000 characters. These are hints, not guaranteed replacements.

    Privacy and cloud models

    GPT and MAI dictation sends audio and dictionary hints to OpenRouter and the model provider. Content retention depends on the service and your account settings.

    How OpenRouter, GPT, and MAI handle your data

    OpenRouter. Does not store inputs or outputs by default. Content logging and use of content to improve its product are opt-in. It keeps request metadata, such as usage and latency, and anonymous categories from sampled prompts. OpenRouter policy.

    GPT-Transcribe. OpenAI lists no abuse-monitoring or application-state retention for its audio transcription endpoint. API content is not used for training unless explicitly shared. OpenAI API policy.

    MAI-Transcribe-2. OpenRouter currently routes this model to Azure. Microsoft's policy says fast transcription does not retain customer audio or transcripts; MAI uses Azure's fast transcription API. This is the documented service policy, rather than a separate guarantee from this app. Microsoft Speech policy.

    Luna cleanup. Sends the transcript, dictionary, and up to 500 nearby characters to OpenRouter and OpenAI. OpenAI's text API may retain content for abuse monitoring for up to 30 days by default, with legal and safety exceptions. Not used for training does not mean not stored.

    Review logging, training, and zero data retention in OpenRouter privacy settings. This app does not enforce zero data retention. These notes cover OpenRouter dictation; MAI streaming through Vercel and direct Grok streaming use separate services and policies.

    Policies checked 3 October 2026. Recordings and transcripts also remain in this app's local History until you delete them.
